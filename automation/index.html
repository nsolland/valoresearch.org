The VALO difference

Automation is easy. Controlled consequences are harder.

A model, agent or workflow does not get a direct path to consequential systems. Authority is resolved at the point of consequence. The outcome is ALLOW, DENY or ESCALATE, with evidence bound to the decision and effect.

01ObserveRead only what the job requires.
02ProposePrepare the next action without granting authority to execute it.
03CheckResolve fresh identity, authority, constraints and evidence.
04ActExecute through the governed effect path only.
05VerifyBind feedback to the exact decision and preserve replayable evidence.

Success is a measured outcome, not an AI demo.

Before building, define the baseline and the acceptance criteria. If the workflow does not produce a verified improvement, stop or change it.
