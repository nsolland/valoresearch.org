Venture Studio · Investment model

We build the companies. Public funding multiplies the deployment capital.

Our venture studio combines original intellectual property, reusable AI technology, operating structures and founder capital with locally executed businesses.

We pursue a strict financing target: the studio supplies no more than 10% of eligible total project capital, while verified, permitted non-dilutive public funding supplies at least 90%. This is a selection rule, not a claim that any particular project has secured funding.

What each side contributes

Capital is only one input.

The studio

IP, AI capabilities, product foundations, company structure, strategy and the required cash contribution.

Local operators

Market access, customer development, hiring, program delivery and independent day-to-day execution. Local equity may range from 0 to 30% where lawful and commercially appropriate.

Public programs

Non-dilutive, project-specific funding for approved costs, subject to eligibility, matching, reporting, disbursement and local presence requirements.

Repeatable establishment

One studio. Multiple local companies.

Discover

Match market needs, technology assets and public programs without favoring an existing product.

Qualify

Verify 90% public coverage, eligible budget, cash timing, funding compatibility and ownership conditions.

Establish

Structure the venture, assign licensed or contributed IP, commit studio capital and recruit a real local team.

Operate

Deliver locally, track evidence, grow revenue and repeat the model only where the financing criteria hold.

Underwriting discipline

What must be proven before capital is committed.

Public financing thresholdAt least 90% of the actual qualifying project budget must be covered by public grants or other permitted non-dilutive public project instruments.

Evidence, not eligibility claimsPublished program rules, funding decisions and signed agreements are distinct; commitments cannot be described as secured before formal approval.

Ownership and local executionTarget studio ownership 70–100%, local team participation 0–30%, subject to actual law, founder roles, IP rights and grant conditions.

Cashflow and compatibilityPre-financing needs, co-funding rules, prohibited double-financing and grant payment schedules must be validated separately.

Investment returnsRevenue, operating margins, venture value and exits are evaluated independently of the public funding multiplier.
