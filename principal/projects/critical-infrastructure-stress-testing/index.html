The project

Observe. Stress. Govern. Reconstruct.

Observe

Use existing sensors, cameras, controllers and operational data instead of assuming greenfield infrastructure.

Stress

Run defined physical and virtual scenarios against real dependencies and operational constraints.

Govern

Route consequential responses through current authority and bounded execution rather than treating an AI recommendation as permission.

Reconstruct

Bind decisions, effects and feedback into evidence that can be replayed after the test or incident.

Technology

IICL + governed consequence path.

IICL provides infrastructure-agnostic observation, state and scenario intelligence. VALO's consequence-governance layer controls whether a proposed response may become a real effect.
