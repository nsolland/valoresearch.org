What Principal delivers

Eight ways to bring the right capability to the work.

Each engagement has a defined scope and a clear output. Principal offers candidates for the lead principal role and assembles specialist capacity around the mandate when needed.

01 · COMPANY & TECHNOLOGY PRINCIPAL

Principal / Interim Company & Technology

Temporary senior capacity for companies that need strategy, technology, economics and execution brought under one accountable mandate.

Work includes
  • Establishing the actual company position, constraints and decision space
  • Technical control, product and architecture direction, operating model and automation
  • Investor readiness, company building, roadmap, execution and handover
Output: a controlled operating picture, decision map and executable company-and-technology plan with clear ownership.
02 · INVESTOR READINESS & CAPITAL

Investor Scope

Prepare a company for investor scrutiny before the fundraising process starts.

Work includes
  • Actual status, capital need, valuation basis and use of funds
  • Investor readiness, evidence, risk, narrative and data-room gaps
  • Investor fit, process design and concrete financing options
Output: a decision-ready investor scope showing what is investable now, what must be fixed first and how the capital process should proceed.
03 · EXECUTIVE & INTERIM LEADERSHIP

Interim CTO / CSO

Senior technical or strategic leadership during growth, transition, restructuring or a leadership gap.

Work includes
  • Technology direction, architecture and product priorities
  • Engineering, supplier and delivery structure
  • Board and investor readiness; handover to permanent leadership
Output: a 30/60/90-day operating plan, decision map and accountable leadership cadence.
04 · INVESTMENT & DUE DILIGENCE

PE Front-End Intelligence

A decision-ready origination layer between an investment mandate and the PE deal team.

Work includes
  • Mandate scoping, market and cluster mapping, target discovery
  • Financial and ownership screening, control feasibility and investment thesis construction
  • Owner and decision-maker mapping with qualified case handover
Output: a prepared investment opportunity handed into the investor’s deal process. First formal contact and transaction execution can remain with the investor. Technical DD is a separate mandate.
05 · TRANSFORMATION & DELIVERY

Programme leadership

Hands-on control of technical or industrial work where execution is delayed, fragmented or poorly owned.

Work includes
  • Programme design, dependencies and decision rights
  • Cross-functional and supplier coordination
  • Recovery of delayed initiatives and escalation of blocked decisions
Output: a delivery structure, visible milestones and evidence of completed gates.
06 · TECHNOLOGY & AI

AI automation, architecture & Handlingsrett

Turn software, industrial systems and AI opportunities into controlled, measurable operations.

Work includes
  • Software, edge, physical AI and compute architecture
  • Process mapping, workflow, agentic and back-office automation
  • Authority, delegation, human oversight and evidence for consequential actions
Output: an opportunity map, target architecture and bounded implementation with measurable acceptance criteria.
07 · TRAINING & CAPABILITY BUILDING

Executive workshops & team training

Applied training for boards, leaders and teams making real decisions about AI and technology.

Work includes
  • Board and executive briefings
  • AI opportunity and governance workshops
  • Role-specific operating practice and tool use
Output: decisions, prioritised use cases and a practical next action for the organisation.
08 · SCALING & COMMERCIALISATION

Franchise, product & partner growth

Build a repeatable operating model around a product, service or proven local business.

Work includes
  • Franchise and partner structure; central versus local control
  • Unit economics, replication and service productisation
  • Industrial suppliers, channels and international expansion
Output: a scalable operating model with economics, quality controls and an expansion path.
Engagement model

Scope the result before staffing the work.

Principal can lead a fixed project, an interim role or a continuous intelligence mandate. Each proposal states the decision or outcome sought, who owns delivery, the evidence required and how the work is paid.

We can assemble technology, AI, industrial, commercial and operational specialists around a lead principal. Specialist capacity is assigned to the mandate, not sold as an anonymous pool of hours.

Interim leadershipDefined weekly or monthly executive responsibility.
Fixed scopeAssessment, technical DD, architecture or delivery setup.
RetainerOngoing senior advice, intelligence or value creation.
Outcome linkedDefined transaction or verified value where appropriate.

Investor Scope is company-side preparation before fundraising. PE Front-End Intelligence is investor-side origination and qualification. For PE Front-End Intelligence, scope, first owner contact, target attribution and compensation are agreed before detailed handover. Principal can create owner access and arrange a qualified meeting where mandated; the investor controls the transaction process after handover.
