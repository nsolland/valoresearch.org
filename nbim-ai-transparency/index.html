NBIM AI Transparency

A source-based record of questions, access requests and responses concerning AI governance at NBIM and Norges Bank.

The question

As AI and software become more operationally significant in managing Norway’s financial wealth, what documented controls govern the path from software to consequence?

This page does not allege that NBIM’s systems are unsafe. It records what has been asked, disclosed, refused and left unresolved.

What was requested

September 2026 requests sought documentation concerning authority and human approval for AI in investment, trading and risk processes; AI-related incidents and near-misses; cases where AI changed investment, trading, risk or compliance outcomes; and the AI News Desk and Agent Factory.

Timeline

15 September 2026 · Norges Bank · ref. 26/04862-68

Access request refused

Norges Bank grouped the requests as concerning its use of AI or governance of AI-related risk. It stated that processing them would require extensive manual mapping and review of documents and that the combined resource and time requirement exceeded what it considered reasonable under section 28, second paragraph, of the Freedom of Information Act. The requests were refused on that basis.

Primary source: Norges Bank, Svar på innsynskrav, 15.09.2026, ref. 26/04862-68.

16 September 2026

Appeal received

Norges Bank confirmed receipt of an appeal concerning case 26/04862-68.

17 September 2026

Targeted request: AI News Desk and Agent Factory

A separate request sought documents concerning the establishment, development and use of the publicly discussed systems.

21 September 2026

Agent Factory follow-up

A follow-up was sent after further public discussion of Agent Factory.

25 September 2026

Status requested

A further request asked for status and expected processing time for outstanding requests and the appeal.

Evidence boundary

Established: detailed access requests were made; Norges Bank refused the grouped September requests because it said processing them would require excessive manual work; an appeal and targeted requests followed.

Not established: that NBIM lacks appropriate controls, that AI autonomously executes investment decisions, or that an AI system has caused financial harm.

Unresolved question

As more analysis and operational work becomes software-mediated, can the public independently understand and verify the authority, permissions, human intervention, execution boundaries, logging and effects governing those systems?

This record will be updated when substantive responses or additional primary-source evidence are received.
