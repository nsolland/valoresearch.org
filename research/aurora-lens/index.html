Research question

What may enter governed state before anything becomes consequential?

How should an AI system determine which evidence objects, interpretations and communications may enter governed persistent state and become candidates for consequence?

Independent architectures

Developed independently. Connected at a defined boundary.

Aurora-Lens

Governed evidence admission

Aurora-Lens governs evidence admission, persistent PEF state, provenance, authority and freshness, contradiction preservation, unresolved references, text and voice communication admissibility, and lawful continuation.

VALO

Execution context and authorisation

VALO adds authenticated identity, mandate and delegation, execution context, consequence and operational state before REHT makes the separate final authorisation decision and Veritas preserves verifiable receipts.

Architectural relationship

Admission is not authorisation.

The collaboration defines a directional relationship without combining ownership or granting one architecture authority over the other.

Non-reversal invariant

Downstream systems cannot manufacture admissibility or authority.

Aurora NON_ADMIT is final downstream.

A rejected evidence object, interpretation or candidate communication cannot be admitted by VALO, REHT or an execution component.

Aurora ADMIT grants no execution authority.

Admission only creates a candidate for further consideration. REHT may still modify, defer, deny, step up or halt the resulting action or release.
