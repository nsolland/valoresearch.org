Universal Retrofit Intelligence

Make existing physical infrastructure locally intelligent instead of replacing it.

Built for the installed world.
Core proposition

Small devices. Local intelligence. Real impact.

Most physical infrastructure already exists. Buildings, motors, cabinets, switches, meters, doors and machines do not need to be replaced just to participate in an intelligent system. A retrofit intelligence layer can add sensing, perception and bounded action at the edge.

The architecture separates simple endpoints from shared intelligence and consequence control. The objective is not to make every device expensive. It is to make the installed world addressable, observable and governable.

Node family

Three node types. One architecture.

Sense NodeAlways-on low-cost sensing for vibration, temperature, current and motion. Detect drift, wake when needed and report what matters.
Perception NodeLocal vision, audio and OCR. Observe, classify and summarize without shipping every raw signal away from the site.
Action NodeGoverned actuation through relay, valve, switch or machine interface. Authorize, act and prove.
Local edge flow

From signal to governed action.

SenseCapture signals from the real world.
UnderstandTurn signals into structured meaning locally.
GovernCheck current authority before consequence.
ActTrigger the bounded real-world effect.
ProveCreate a receipt and synchronize when needed.

Only what matters needs to leave the device. The rest can happen locally.

Applications

Upgrade what already exists.

Smart buildingsModernize switches, rooms and shared spaces without full infrastructure replacement.
Industrial equipmentMonitor machines, detect drift and improve uptime around installed assets.
Utility & energyAdd local awareness to cabinets, meters and field assets.
Access & safetySee events locally and trigger only bounded, authorized action.
Retail & facilitiesObserve, alert and summarize locally while reducing unnecessary data movement.
Field operationsOperate with intermittent connectivity and local autonomy where central services are unavailable.
Why it matters

Retrofit changes the economics.

Faster deploymentRetrofit instead of redesign.
Local privacyKeep sensitive data at the edge where possible.
Lower upgrade costReuse the installed base.
SustainabilityExtend asset life and reduce replacement waste.
Architecture principle

Intelligence where useful. Governance where consequential.

Edge intelligence should not mean uncontrolled autonomy. Local interpretation and local action can reduce latency, bandwidth and cloud dependence, but consequential effects still require a bounded authority path and evidence of what actually happened.

Status: concept architecture and applied research direction. Device, cost, performance and interoperability claims require hardware-specific validation.
