Frozen rules / criteria
Different intelligences are allowed to pursue “best” differently under the same physical rules.

One team may pursue the most extraordinary single fruit. Another may maximize Grade-01 conversion. Another may optimize taste per kWh, consistency, resource efficiency, robustness or realized market value.

The objective is frozen before execution. A competitor cannot redefine success after seeing the outcome.

This exposes a Pareto frontier of biological production strategies instead of pretending there is one universally optimal way to grow a melon.

Prioritymust become an observable criterion before the run.
Criterionmust have a named metric, measurement source, unit or scale, target/direction, evaluation point, aggregation rule, tie-break and hard constraints.
Evidencemust support the score. Post-hoc metric selection is invalid.

Every valid run receives an identity before execution. Every melon carries lineage and provenance. Every data centre accumulates seasons, strategies, records, rivalries and championships.

The public can follow the season and ask the AI what it did and why.

observation → prediction → proposed action → authority → executed action → measured consequence → updated state

If the evidence does not establish the reason, the system says it is unknown.

Community
Give the data centre something alive to care for — and something the public can care about.

The season can run in public. Employees, schools, chefs and local communities can follow their team, question the AI, watch predictions meet consequences and build rivalries across seasons.

Competition fruit goes to grading and finals. A controlled subset can test real market value. Numbered supporter fruit can go to followers; remaining valid fruit can be shared with employees and teams.

The melon does not need to pay for the data centre. The hypothesis is that it may make the data centre more valuable to the people around it. That value must be measured, not assumed.
