VALO 30

Not just a house.
A physical platform.

VALO 30 is the first home in the Habitat series: a complete home of up to about 30 m² that can change through its lifetime. The architecture separates what should endure from what must adapt to climate, place and use.

01Primary structure

Frame, structure, lifting points, technical core, openings and fixed connection points.

02Regional climate envelope

Exterior cladding and insulation adapted to Nordic, Mediterranean, tropical, desert or remote environments.

03Climate module

Replaceable heating, cooling, ventilation, filtration and humidity control.

04Technical package

Kitchen, bathroom, electricity, water, data and interior elements that can be adapted.

05Roof system

Removable infrastructure for solar panels, walkways, technical systems and future functions.

Technical system · proposal

Built for the site.
Anchored to the ground.

VALO30 can be factory-produced and transported to the site. VALO30 Foundation is a dedicated permanent product system sized for VALO30, covering foundation, anchoring, load transfer, drainage, frost protection and the ground interface. Ground conditions and execution are still engineered for the specific site.

01 · HomeVALO30

Factory-built home with documented structural, fire, moisture, indoor-climate, energy and technical systems for year-round use. Fixed interface to VALO30 Foundation.

02 · SubstructureVALO30 Foundation

Permanent VALO30-specific solution for load-bearing, mechanical anchoring, load transfer, drainage, frost and the ground interface. Wind and uplift, moisture, corrosion and inspection must also be documented. It is not a universal foundation for other modules.

03 · UtilitiesInfrastructure Module

Modular solutions for water, wastewater, electricity, energy, battery, fibre, stormwater and waste. The expansion tank remains here and is sized for the selected installation. Parking, shared functions and later extensions scale with the site, capacity and need.

04 · Site rightSite/Leasehold Module

Owned land or a documented leasehold, with duration, use rights, transfer and security assessed for the specific case. A leasehold is a right to land, not a foundation.

05 · ComplianceCompliance Module

Clarify zoning and any exemptions, apply for building permission, document relevant TEK17 requirements and close the building case with a completion certificate or temporary occupancy permit. The municipality assesses the specific project.

VALO30 Foundation is a dedicated VALO30-specific SKU. Excavation and site preparation are a separate delivery: ground investigation, excavation where required, fill, drainage and documentation of the completed ground. The Foundation delivery includes drawings for the VALO30 interface, load calculations, anchoring plan, installation and inspection plan, and documentation of completed work. Foundation type, dimensions and execution must be engineered for the site. The VALO30 + VALO30 Foundation core is intended as a permanent home after installation and municipal approval. Other infrastructure and shared capacity can be added modularly. This does not automatically grant building permission or eligibility for a Norwegian start loan.

DiBK describes the Norwegian micro-home rules for a detached year-round home up to 30 m² BRA, one dwelling unit, one storey, no basement and no more than 4.5 m above ground. See the rules at DiBK →

The Norwegian State Housing Bank allows stationary micro-homes permanently built into the ground to be assessed. A start loan is not a general product approval; the municipality also assesses the applicant, suitability, value and security. For new construction, the start loan cannot be paid out before a completion certificate or temporary occupancy permit is available. Lifecycle architecture

Repair by replacing.
Upgrade without rebuilding.

Habitat is designed with a structure intended to last and technical systems that can be replaced more frequently. Energy, water, wastewater, climate, controls, kitchen and parts of the interior should be replaceable as modules.

Repairable

A failed technical module should be removable and replaceable with minimal interruption.

Upgradeable

New battery, solar, water, climate and control technology can be adopted without replacing the home.

Shared spare parts

An operator with multiple homes can keep standard spare parts available across all sites.

Habitat Service

Maintenance, remote monitoring, replacement modules and planned upgrades can be offered as an ongoing service.

Global platform · local products

Same system architecture.
Different physical products.

VALO30 is the Norwegian product configuration of Habitat. The roughly 30 m², single-storey and Norwegian height limits are Norwegian regulatory and economic optimisations — not global constraints. In other countries, area, height, storeys, geometry, structure and materials can be re-optimised for local rules, climate, market, production and logistics.

Possible organisational models

Different sites.
Different agreements.

Habitat can be organised differently from site to site. VALO30 can be owned by the resident on owned or leased land, or form part of a jointly owned housing project. Housing cooperatives, condominiums, cooperatives and shared operating companies are options that must be assessed against the site, financing, use and ownership of shared facilities. None of these models is a standard or confirmed model for Habitat.

Individual home

The resident owns VALO30 with a documented right to the land. Shared benefits can be agreed separately so the home does not depend on everyone choosing the same services.

Housing cooperative or condominium

Can be considered for a combined housing site where ownership of homes, land, access and common areas is organised together. The legal form must be assessed for the specific property.

Cooperative across sites

A possible model for shared equipment, modules and services. Farmers and other landowners can also consider portfolio financing, but ownership, security and revenue sharing must be agreed separately.

Cost per home

Road, electricity, water, wastewater and necessary maintenance have fixed costs. Show them separately from optional shared benefits and calculate cost per home even at low unit counts.

Use and capacity

Car pool, sauna, kitchen, Studio 10/20 and guest rooms require capacity at peak demand while idle capacity also costs. Introduce them when use and financing can be justified.

Operational responsibility

Clarify booking, cleaning, repair, insurance and who pays for damage before a shared benefit opens. User payments can cover use, but fixed costs must also be financed.

Shared operations can be organised per site or across several sites. Revenue from external users or rentals is an option, not a base-budget assumption until demand and permissions are clarified.

Purchase model

Five parts.
One understandable agreement.

Habitat can be established on owned or leased land. VALO30 with its Foundation, site preparation, right to the land, infrastructure, recurring operations and financing must therefore be described and priced separately. This is a proposed model, not a completed purchase offer or loan commitment.

01 · HomeHabitat

Price and specification for the home itself: structure, VALO30 Foundation, delivery, installation, base equipment, warranty and responsibility for replacement or relocation.

02 · LandLeasehold

A standardised agreement for long-term use of the land should clarify rights, duration, ground rent and adjustment, transfer, lender security and what happens on termination.

03 · ConnectionsInfrastructure

Separate price and agreement for road and access, electricity, water, wastewater, fibre, waste, parking and common facilities. Ownership, operating responsibility, capacity and cost sharing must be explicit.

04 · RecurringOperations and shared costs

Visible costs for maintenance, insurance, service, common areas and any shared services. The agreement should show who delivers and pays for what.

05 · CapitalFinancing

Home, leasehold and infrastructure can be assessed separately by a lender. Lending options and security depend on specific agreements, ownership, project approvals and the bank’s assessment.

The goal is for the buyer to see the total establishment cost and recurring cost before deciding, while the landowner and financing party have clear rights and obligations. No financing arrangement has yet been confirmed for Habitat.

From concept to delivery

What must be ready before purchase?

VALO30 is a home core that can stand alone. The Habitat network with multiple homes and shared benefits is a separate project track for landowners, operators and municipalities. Both need a specific site, clear delivery responsibility and a verifiable cost basis before sale.

One site case

Check land rights, zoning, access, ground conditions, connections and municipal processing for one actual pilot. No approved Habitat site has been announced.

One complete cost

Separate the factory price of VALO30 from Foundation, site preparation, transport and installation, connections, public fees and recurring operations. State the source and status of each amount.

Responsibility at every interface

Clarify who engineers, manufactures, transports, installs, connects, documents and handles defects before a turnkey offer can be made.

Evidence before claims

Production partner, lead time and price must be confirmed by agreement. Building products need relevant documented properties; the completed home and site must be documented against applicable requirements and the building case.
