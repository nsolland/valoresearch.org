Physical capability network

Space that changes with need.

VALO Habitat applies composable-system architecture to the physical world. A stable structural core connects replaceable climate, energy, compute, connectivity and living capabilities.

VALO 30

Not one house.
A physical platform.

VALO 30 is the first habitat product: a complete autonomous home up to approximately 30 m² that can change configuration over its lifetime. The architecture separates what should remain stable from what must vary by climate, market and use.

01Base Shell

Chassis, structure, lifting points, service core, openings and stable connection datums.

02Regional Shell

Nordic, Mediterranean, Tropical, Desert or Remote envelope configuration.

03Climate

Replaceable heating, cooling, ventilation, filtration and humidity capability.

04Service Pack

Kitchen, bath, electrical, water, data and configurable interior.

05Roof Grid

Removable infrastructure for PV, pathways, services, technical equipment and future capabilities.

Lifecycle architecture

Repair by replacement.
Upgrade without rebuilding.

Habitat is designed for a long-lived structural core and short-cycle technical systems. Energy, water, wastewater, climate, controls, kitchen and selected interior systems should be replaceable as modules rather than treated as permanent building fabric.

Serviceable

A failed technical module should be removable and replaceable with minimum downtime.

Upgradeable

New battery, solar, water, climate and control technology can be added without replacing the home.

Shared spares

Multi-unit operators can hold common spare modules instead of unique parts for every site.

Habitat Service

Maintenance, remote monitoring, replacement modules and planned upgrades become a recurring service layer.

Regional by design

One core.
Different worlds.

Norway is the first demanding reference configuration, not the definition of the product. The same core is intended to accept different climate and compliance packs without redesigning the chassis and interfaces.

Deployment

From one home
to a network.

The same physical grammar scales from an individual dwelling to sites where residential, shared and technical modules can be rearranged as demand changes.

Rental village

Phased capacity, repeatable operations and relocatable assets.

Farm stay network

Farmers can turn suitable land and landscape into recurring hospitality income without each building a separate operating system.

Eco camping

Premium small-format accommodation with off-grid and shared-service options.

Student housing

Compact private units combined with shared social and service capability.

Short-term housing

Rapid, reconfigurable capacity for projects, workforce and transitional demand.

Hospitality / Airbnb

Standardized operations with differentiated location, facade and interior packs.

Mixed habitat

Homes, workspace, energy, compute and shared facilities on one interface system.

Habitat cooperative

Own the network together.
Finance the portfolio together.

A group of farmers or landowners can form a cooperative or shared operating company that owns and finances a portfolio of Habitat units. Members contribute equity and sites; the operating entity can use the pooled equity as the basis for portfolio financing rather than requiring every member to finance one unit alone.

Pooled equity

Members contribute a defined equity stake instead of carrying the full investment individually.

Portfolio finance

Debt can be raised against the operating entity, asset pool and documented cash flow, subject to lender terms and security.

Shared operations

Booking, marketing, insurance, service, spare modules and upgrades can be handled once for the whole network.

Local income

Each host contributes land and local operation and receives compensation through an agreed site and revenue-sharing model.

Reinvestment

Cash flow can support debt service, reserves and expansion into additional units as performance becomes documented.

Portable assets

Standardized units can be reallocated between sites as demand, permits or economics change.

The building becomes composable infrastructure.

Habitat combines a durable physical core, replaceable capability modules, lifecycle service and portfolio ownership. One unit can be a home. Many units can become a distributed operating network.
