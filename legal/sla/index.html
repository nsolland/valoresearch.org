Service Levels

Effective 26 September 2026 · Operator: Njål Gaute Solland, Norway

Public services

Public websites, research surfaces and demos have no uptime, recovery-time or support SLA. They may change, be interrupted or be withdrawn.

Pilots

Pilot support and response expectations are best effort unless the pilot agreement states specific service levels.

Production

Production availability, support windows, severity definitions, response targets, recovery targets and service credits apply only when explicitly agreed in writing for that service.

Measurement

Where a production SLA is agreed, the agreement must define the measured service boundary, measurement source, exclusions and reporting period.

Exclusions

Unless a production agreement states otherwise, maintenance, customer-caused failures, third-party failures outside the agreed service boundary, force majeure and misuse are not represented as covered availability.
