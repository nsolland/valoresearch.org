Delegated Authority for AI Agents

When an AI agent acts, the real question is not only who called it. The question is who it represents, what authority was delegated, and what limits apply.

AI agents act through delegation. A user, team, system or organization asks the agent to perform work. That work may affect customers, data, money, operations, compliance or safety.

Delegated authority defines the right-to-act relationship between the human or organization and the AI agent.

The delegation problem

An AI agent can have valid identity, valid credentials and valid tool access while still lacking legitimate authority to perform a specific action.

What must be known

  • Who initiated the request?
  • Who does the agent represent?
  • What objective was delegated?
  • What policy limits apply?
  • What context is true now?
  • When does the authority expire?
  • What proof must be created?

Authority chain

An authority chain links the actor, role, delegation, policy, context, execution decision and receipt.

Human or organization -> delegated role -> AI agent -> proposed action -> VALO eXeC decision -> receipt -> outcome -> gain

Why authorization is not enough

Authorization usually answers whether a subject can access a resource or call a tool.

Delegated authority asks a richer question: should this agent perform this action on behalf of this person or organization, for this purpose, under this policy, right now?

VALO eXeC framing

VALO eXeC treats authority as part of execution control, not as a static permission table.

  • Authority can be scoped.
  • Authority can expire.
  • Authority can require quorum.
  • Authority can require escalation.
  • Authority can be invalidated by context.

FAQ

What is delegated authority for AI agents?

It is the defined right for an AI agent to act on behalf of a person, team or organization within a specific scope.

Why does this matter?

Because agents can create real-world consequences. The organization must know whether the agent had authority before the action happened.

Is delegated authority the same as user consent?

No. Consent may be one input. Delegated authority also includes role, policy, context, scope, expiry, risk and proof requirements.

How does VALO use delegated authority?

VALO checks delegated authority before execution and records proof after the decision.
