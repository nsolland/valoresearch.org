Author of The Eukaryotic Transition in Artificial Intelligence, Φ — Den neste setningen, and Spes Phthisica Digitalis. Inventor of the Law of Identity Maintenance (LIM) and builder of VAIG — the Validation AI Guard.

The founding incident: an AI system nearly killed a patient. Caught only because the doctor had reference books. VALO started there — not as a philosophical project but as an engineering response to a real failure mode.
