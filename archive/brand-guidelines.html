Tone of Voice

VALO speaks directly, without jargon. Outcome-first, not explanation-first.

✓ DO

Direct: "Load AI. Load VALO. Run. ROI Found."

Outcome-focused: Start with the result, not the mechanism.

Honest: "Either it works or it doesn't."

Technical when needed: Explain clearly, not showily.

✗ DON'T

Jargon-heavy: Avoid "admissibility enforcement judge" as opening line.

Explanation-first: Don't explain how it works before showing the outcome.

Salesy: Not "revolutionary AI governance solution"—just "ROI you can prove."

Vague: Be specific. Show receipts, proof, evidence.
