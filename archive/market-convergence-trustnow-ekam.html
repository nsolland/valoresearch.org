Market convergence / adjacent competitor

TRUSTNOW.EKAM

Observed 31 August 2026 · Express Computer

TRUSTNOW has launched EKAM, a sovereign AI-governance platform for autonomous enterprise agents. The product combines agent discovery, cryptographic identity, least-privilege access, real-time policy enforcement, allow/escalate/block decisions, a kill switch, and auditable records of agent activity and authorisation.

Market signal: governance is moving from static policy and post-hoc monitoring toward real-time control at the point where agents act.

VALO overlap: identity, scoped authority, runtime decisions, escalation/blocking, auditability, model independence, and enterprise deployment boundaries.

Current distinction: the public EKAM description is primarily identity/access + runtime policy + observability. It does not establish fresh authority resolution at consequence time, a single governed effect path / NO_DIRECT_EFFECT_PATH, or deterministic evidence and replay of the executed effect.

Positioning

EKAM is a near neighbour and direct market-convergence signal, not evidence that the VALO execution-governance problem has been solved elsewhere. Track ONE TRUST FABRIC and EKAM closely, especially any claims covering commit-time authority, effect-path enforcement, execution receipts, replay, or governed inter-agent delegation.
