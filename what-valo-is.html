Canonical explanation

VALO Research is not the governance layer.

VALO Research develops, tests and operates systems around consequential work, economic units, evidence and execution.

Heimel is the governance and authority infrastructure that decides whether consequential action is authorized now.

The boundary

Research and operation on one side. Authority infrastructure on the other.

VALO can build products, run experiments, assemble GCUs, evaluate evidence, test economic models and publish operative systems. When one of those systems needs consequence-time authority, that responsibility belongs to Heimel.

Simple rule: if the question is what VALO is, the answer is research and operative systems. If the question is who governs consequential authority, the answer is Heimel.
Operating model

Need → Build → Execute → Prove → Transfer.

VALO Research starts with real work. It builds enough system to run the claim, measures what survives contact with reality, and transfers the result into reusable products, contracts and knowledge.
