Trusted Execution

Trusted Execution is not a claim about a model. It is an evidenced property of an outcome produced through a governed effect path.

Trust belongs to the execution chain.

A system reaches Trusted Execution when the proposed consequence was evaluated against current authority and evidence, the decision was enforced before effect, and a receipt preserves what was evaluated, decided and executed or refused.

Trusted Execution = current authorization + enforced effect path + replayable evidence.

What it does not mean

It does not mean the model is universally trustworthy, that prior approval remains valid forever, or that observability alone is sufficient. It is bounded to the evidenced execution instance.
