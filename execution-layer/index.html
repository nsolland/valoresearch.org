The action boundary

No machine-caused consequence without a fresh, verifiable right to act.

Intelligence → intent → risk & admissibility → fresh Handlingsrett → governed execution → consequence → economics & evidence → governed learning.

The stack around this boundary may change. Models, agent runtimes, clouds, hardware and external effect providers may be replaced. The Handlingsrett boundary remains. Prediction is not mandate. Risk and admissibility do not grant authority. Economic value does not grant authority.
