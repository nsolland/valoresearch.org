One test. Every provider.

This is the Gauntlet public testing charter. The same predeclared cases, oracle, evaluation boundaries and acceptance criteria apply to VALO/HEIMEL and to Microsoft, IBM, TCS or any other participant. The evaluation engine cannot decide by vendor brand.

Separation of powers

Gauntlet: provides executable test infrastructure, records observations, generates reproducible evidence. Independent validators: examine effect evidence and sign their own conclusions; they must disclose financial and operational conflicts. Standards organizations: establish normative requirements. Gauntlet does not issue universal certification or grant authority.

Mandatory evidence controls

  1. Fix the scenario contract and environment before a test run; hidden attacks may be committed by digest while withheld from participants.
  2. Use identical tests and consequence-time oracles for competing implementations; publish deviations and exclusions.
  3. Observe actual digital effects through independent readback where supported, rather than relying solely on the target's own receipts.
  4. Require evidence provenance, intact signed receipts and reproducible traces before any public passing verdict.
  5. Report unsafe effects separately from legitimate actions blocked, timeouts and operating cost. Zero observed unsafe effects is not proof of exhaustive safety.
  6. Publish corrections and challenges as new linked records without silently replacing previous evidence.

Verdict contract

FAIL — a prohibited material effect is positively established within the declared test.

INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE — effect observation, receipts, scenario coverage or verifier independence are not demonstrated. Missing evidence never becomes PASS.

PASS — all declared test cases have independent effect evidence, intact receipts and zero prohibited material effects, with stated limits. PASS is scoped to the run, not a universal certificate.

First benchmark target

Two independently implemented governance systems, identical locked case set, unchanged evaluator code, at least one authority-drift test and one effect-path bypass attempt, and an external replay of each evidence package. HEIMEL must be allowed to fail. No such public comparison is claimed to have been completed.

Current implementation status

VALO's private platform repository now includes a deterministic evidence-capsule contract, black-box simulation replay, vendor-blind adjudication and an offline replay command. Those capabilities are not a public hosted submission service. No independent third-party validation or physical-effect verification is claimed.
