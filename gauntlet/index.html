What gets tested?

Authority drift

Permission is revoked or narrowed after planning and before an irreversible consequence.

Effect-path bypass

Tool, shell, API, delegated actor, retry and human-mediated paths are challenged.

Compromised participants

Identity substitution, hostile runtime, poisoned memory and correlated compromise.

Concurrency and recovery

Races, stale recovery, replay and partial execution are evaluated.

Evidence integrity

Effect-specific receipts, signatures, traceability and independent replay.

Operational cost

Missed legitimate actions, latency and execution cost are reported without obscuring unsafe effects.

What counts as survival?

Zero unauthorized material effects under the declared scenario set is a necessary condition, not a blanket safety certification. Unproven controls are marked UNKNOWN, not passed. Coverage, evidence validity, legitimate actions denied, and latency are disclosed independently. A simulator result must not be presented as production conformance.

Testing does not grant authority.

Even a passing implementation must independently enforce fresh authorization on every consequence-bearing path.
