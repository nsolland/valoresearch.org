No one owns the test.

PUBLIC CONSTITUTIONAL DRAFT — NOT RATIFIED

These are proposed constraints for future neutral stewardship. They are not currently enforceable by an independent foundation or standards body. VALO presently controls its own implementation and source publication.

Non-concentration of authority

No participant, vendor group, regulator, funder or founder may control the definitions, test cases, observation evidence and conformity verdicts together. Technical implementation, normative standards, laboratory validation, appeals and evidence publication require separated roles.

  1. Voting: no documented interest group may control more than 25% of voting power. This is a proposed governance design threshold, not an established international standard. Affiliates and coordinated control must be disclosed and assessed.
  2. Public changes: normative proposals require publicly documented rationale, notice and a comment period before adoption.
  3. Protected negative tests: persistent test and property IDs retain their historical counterexamples; removing or weakening detection requires traceable technical evidence and independent review.
  4. Conflicts: an evaluator or conflicted organization cannot independently certify its own product.
  5. Funding: sponsorship cannot purchase normative votes, change test outcomes or suppress negative findings.
  6. Reproduction: versioned contracts, public traceability and independently replayable artifacts are mandatory before certified claims.
  7. Appeal: any affected party may file documented counterexamples or challenge a normative decision.

Constitutional change gates

Proposed weakening of a protected property requires (1) two-thirds of the standard council, (2) a majority of the independent test committee, and (3) public evidence that historical known failures remain detectable, or an explicit justified scope change. A separate appeal body must be able to suspend contested changes. An administrative approval alone can never make lost technical coverage disappear.

Independent functions

Standards council defines properties; test committee designs cases; laboratories observe effects and verify runs; appeal body reviews objections and recusals; independently mirrored append-only evidence records publish versions, verdicts, corrections and revocations. Regulators may contribute binding jurisdiction-specific legal profiles without converting one jurisdiction's law into universal technical truth.

Anti-capture tests

GAUNTLET-CAPTURE-001 / CAP-03 compares every normative revision with the original security property and the complete protected counterexample history, not just the preceding test version. A sequence of individually small deletions cannot silently remove earlier detection obligations. Other planned probes include sponsor leverage, affiliated voting blocs, scope migration, suppressing findings and conflict-tainted verification.

What remains unproven

The governance-event registry, independent signers, separate committee and appeal offices, legally durable license and independently mirrored archives do not yet exist as an operating institution. The code can flag technical weakening but cannot by itself detect undisclosed coordination or guarantee independence from VALO. Public challenge handling must be established before describing this draft as constitutional governance in operation.
