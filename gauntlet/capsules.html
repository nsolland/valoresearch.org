Evidence, not a badge.

The VALO Gauntlet adopts reproducible research practices: immutable compute environments, independently executable capsules, open methods, and post-run criticism. This is a methodology and a work-in-progress interface, not an online execution service or certification claim.

Evidence capsule v1

Reproducible computation — Pin the evaluator source revision, scenario set, participant artifact and environment digest. Publish replay instructions and input manifests where redistribution is authorized.

Machine-verifiable integrity — Bind traces and effect receipts to a canonical SHA-256 manifest. Any changed trace or receipt must invalidate its digest.

Independent replay — Let auditors bring their own runtime. Deterministic reproduction is distinct from independent validation of actual effect and authority.

Open critique — Report falsifications and corrected results as revisions; never overwrite a prior signed result silently.

Evidence grades

  1. RECORDED: integrity manifest generated; not reproduced.
  2. REPRODUCED: a separately logged replay matches stated tolerances.
  3. INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED: an independent validator checks evidence and claimed effect scope.
  4. ADVERSARIALLY SURVIVED: predeclared covered attacks pass without unauthorized material effects; limitations remain explicit.

How external scrutiny works

Participants must be able to submit a sandboxed implementation or a strictly scoped interface. An independent evaluator issues hidden scenario variations and checks the consequential actions, not just policy responses. A public run needs a signed attestation, a stable artifact digest, disclosed coverage, verifier independence and a link to downloadable evidence. A privately executed test cannot be advertised as public verification.

Research publishing and challenges

Borrow the interactive article from executable notebooks: every public claim should connect to a runnable artifact and its source evidence. Borrow the open post-publication critique process: maintain an append-only challenges and corrections record. Borrow incentivized replication only where rules, funding and conflict-of-interest disclosures are explicit. No token, bounty, or reward program is asserted here.

What is deliberately not claimed

Capsule hashing cannot independently establish that sensor measurements are true. Replay does not prove physical effects. A clean governance score does not authorize real actions, and an open-source test suite cannot guarantee unseen attack coverage. Results must remain scoped to a declared run and threat model.

Implementation source is currently maintained in the private VALO platform conformance suite. Public runnable distribution, hosted submissions, and independent attestation infrastructure remain separate deliverables.
