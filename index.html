Research in action / HEIMEL

Authority at the moment of consequence.

For teams using AI and automation, a good proposal is not enough. HEIMEL checks current authority before an action takes effect, and leaves evidence of the decision. REHT is the authorization mechanism underneath.

01Intent
02Fresh authority
03Permit

If authority changes, the action can be stopped at the point of effect. Inspect the decision and the resulting evidence in a public demonstration.

Physical systems / VALO Habitat

Built for places, not just screens.

Habitat starts with one standard VALO30 home. The site, foundation, infrastructure and financing are assessed together so a buyer can see the full cost of moving in. The film shows a concept, not a built project.
