Research through operation

Do not take our word for it. Run the work.

VALO Research starts with a consequential need, governs the path to action, executes under explicit constraints, captures evidence, and transfers what survives back into the platform.

All consequential authority.Heimel is authority infrastructure for any actor that can create consequence — human, AI, software, workflow, API, robot or organization. Actor type is secondary. The control question is whether the exact action is authorized now, under current authority, constraints and state.
