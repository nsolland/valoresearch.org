Ways to contribute

Clear roles. Bounded commitments.

The relationship should be legible before the work begins.

Research collaborator

Work on a defined question

Jointly define scope, methods, artifacts, authorship and publication expectations.

Independent reviewer

Test claims without joining

Review methods, evidence, architecture or terminology. Critical findings count as useful output.

University partner

Build formal research work

Supervision, student projects, formal methods, evaluation and independent replication.

Benchmark contributor

Make evaluation harder

Provide tasks, datasets or evaluation methods that expose real failure boundaries.

Affiliated researcher

Join a bounded program

Contribute while retaining institutional identity and transparent disclosure.

Visiting fellow

Push one line of inquiry

A time-bounded collaboration around a question that benefits from close access to the work.

Process

Question → contract → evidence.

A useful first message is short: the question, why it matters, what you can test or contribute, and what kind of output you expect.
