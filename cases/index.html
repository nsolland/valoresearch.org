What changes at the boundary.

Healthcare

ER Decision Gate

Medical

Problem

AI clinical decision support recommended high-dose medication. Dosage was syntactically valid but inconsistent with patient history: a 78-year-old with renal impairment.

VALO response

VAIG detected coherence collapse. Execution stopped and the decision escalated for review instead of auto-approval.

Outcome

Harm prevented: incorrect dosage was flagged before administration. The patient received the correct dose and the decision trail remained available.

Finance

Trading Limit Violation

Capital Markets

Problem

An AI trading agent recommended a $2M order in a limited market. The trade was syntactically valid but violated the firm's 15-minute liquidity window.

VALO response

Policy incoherence was detected and the trade escalated to the desk manager for explicit override and signature.

Outcome

Regulatory proof: the audit trail showed human approval at the execution boundary and documented authority for the actual trade.

Energy

Grid Stability Gate

Infrastructure

Problem

An AI grid operator recommended ramping down 200MW of wind capacity. The adjustment was technically valid but context-blind to holiday demand and frequency stability.

VALO response

The authority/context mismatch triggered deferral and routed the decision to a human grid controller with the relevant evidence.

Outcome

Cascade prevented: ramp-down was deferred, the controller made the decision with broader context, and grid stability was preserved.

Compliance

Governance

Problem

Corporate

Pattern

Technical correctness is not enough.

What was missing

  • Context — patient history, market conditions, grid state, incident status, calendar conflicts.
  • Authority — verified source, explicit approval, valid mandate or override.
  • Consequence awareness — downstream harm that was not visible in the local task.

The boundary question

Given what we know right now, should this action happen?

If the answer is no or unresolved, effect stops and the decision moves to a stronger authority or evidence state.
