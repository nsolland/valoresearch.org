What These Cases Teach

The Pattern

In every case, the AI was technically correct. Syntax was valid, policy was clear, decision logic was sound. But execution would have failed because:

  • Context was missing — Patient history, market conditions, grid state, incident status, calendar conflicts
  • Authority was incomplete — No human signature at the boundary, no verified request source, no override capability
  • Consequence was hidden — Harm was latent, regulatory exposure was dormant, cascade was non-obvious

VALO's Role

VALO is not authorizing actions. VALO is enforcing admissibility. It answers one question:

"Given what we know right now, should this action happen?"

If the answer is "no" or "maybe," action stops. Human decides. Decision gets recorded. Audit trail proves governance happened.
