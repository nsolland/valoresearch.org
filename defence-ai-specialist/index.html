What we do

Specialist engineering, integrated into your programme.

We support organisations developing dual-use and defence-relevant technologies that need narrow, high-value AI expertise. Our focus is engineering, evidence and practical delivery, not generic consultancy.

Applied AI & model engineering

Model selection, evaluation, adaptation, multi-model systems, inference efficiency and reproducible benchmarking.

Edge & on-device AI

Deploy AI under real constraints in compute, power, latency, bandwidth and intermittent connectivity.

Autonomous systems

Decision architecture, operational-state modelling, safe fallback behaviour, human control and systems integration.

Physical AI & sensing

Perception pipelines, sensor integration, intelligent robotics and the connection between software and the physical environment.

Simulation & scenario testing

Pre-deployment testing, synthetic scenarios, robustness assessment and stress-testing of models under uncertainty.

Verification & reliability

Formal methods, test automation, traceability, quality assurance, auditable AI execution and resilient system design.

Who we support

European programmes need more than consortium membership.

We work as a specialist supplier to prime contractors, SMEs, research teams and existing consortia. Engagement can be structured as a paid feasibility study, defined R&D subcontract, validation work package, reusable technology component or ongoing specialist engineering support.

European Defence Fund (EDF) projects

Technical subcontracting and independently scoped work packages, subject to programme-specific procurement, eligibility and security requirements.

AGILE and rapid defence innovation

Engineering and validation support for qualified innovators pursuing faster technology development. Any direct grant participation is assessed separately from subcontracting.

Commercial and national defence programmes

Direct R&D contracts with European defence suppliers, technology SMEs and systems integrators, including work outside EU-funded programmes.

We are not claiming existing supplier approval, programme funding, defence security accreditation or formal association with EU institutions. Engagement and eligibility are evaluated for each contract.

Engagement model

Start with the missing capability, not a large partnership.

01 — Define the gap

Identify the technical blocker, constraints, acceptance criteria and what your team already has.

02 — Scope a paid work package

Agree a focused feasibility study, prototype, benchmarking exercise or engineering deliverable.

03 — Deliver and verify

Provide testable outputs, evidence, integration guidance and clear boundaries around what has actually been demonstrated.
