Human Control Barometer

Measure the evidence of human control in AI-assisted decisions.

HCB is a standalone retrospective measurement product. It examines review timing, intervention, escalation and independent challenge so teams can inspect what a human review actually involved.

The product

Review should leave measurable evidence.

An approval records a decision. HCB examines the observable review around that decision: time available, interventions, challenges, escalation and independent judgment. Authority evidence can be inspected separately from review behavior.

Observable review

Retrospective measurement of recorded review behavior, intervention and escalation. Missing evidence remains unknown.

Separate control and authority

Behavioral evidence describes the review. HEIMEL resolves current handlingsrett at the consequence boundary. A barometer reading does not grant permission to act.

Human Control Research / Nishita track

Did the right person understand the proposal?

The research programme tests comprehension, independent challenge and reviewer expertise. Teachback asks reviewers to explain a proposal, predict its consequences and identify errors. Code explanations, rationale and provenance can support review; whether they improve understanding must be measured.

Understanding and task fit

Relevant expertise and performance on the actual task are separate research variables. A confident approval alone establishes neither understanding nor competence.

Physiological markers

Gaze, pupil response, HR/HRV, EDA and EEG are candidate research channels. Their added value must be tested against behavior and individual baselines. Physiology is not ground truth.

Product status

Experimental. Retrospective validation.

The current baseline is a software measurement prototype with behavioral HCM integration. It is not presented as a validated clinical system or a finished physiological monitoring product.

No validated physiological inference, prospective control-failure prediction, clinical safety claim or sensor-created authority or fitness decision is claimed. Teachback and reviewer-fit methods remain research work.
Inspect and collaborate

Start with a bounded review study.

The immediate application is retrospective or simulated review of AI-assisted decisions. Clinical simulation and machine-generated code review are research contexts; results must be tied to a defined task, baseline and observed evidence.
