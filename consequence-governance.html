Canonical category

Govern the consequence.
Not the worker.

Consequence Governance decides whether an exact proposed consequence may legitimately become real under the authority, evidence and conditions that exist now. The proposer may be an AI, person, workflow, API, device or machine.

Do not govern thought. Govern the path from capability to consequence.
Category shift

From access mediation to causal mediation.

Zero Trust removed implicit trust from network location and access. Consequence Governance extends the same distrust and re-verification discipline to consequential causal capacity.

Zero Trust

Access

May this subject access this resource now?

Consequence Governance

Effect

May this causal capacity become this consequence now?

Extension

Complete mediation

Verification and enforcement follow every relevant causal path to externally consequential state.

Security model

The intelligence does not have to remain trustworthy.

Reasoning, planning, simulation, disagreement, hallucination and arbitrary internal state may remain untrusted while contained. The control objective is external: untrusted computation may not convert capability into relevant external consequence through an ungoverned causal path.

Internal computation may remain free inside its bounded domain. Its paths to the world may not.
Formal causal invariant
NO_UNGOVERNED_CAUSAL_EFFECT_PATH

No causal path from untrusted computation to a relevant external consequence may succeed unless it crosses an explicit governed effect boundary for the exact effect under current authority.

NO_DIRECT_EFFECT_PATH remains the compatibility identifier used by existing implementation, tests and receipts. NO_UNGOVERNED_CAUSAL_EFFECT_PATH names the complete formal semantics.

Governed boundary

A named gateway is not enough.

Explicit + bound

The boundary is declared and the decision binds the exact proposed consequence.

Authorized + enforceable

Authority is current, and DENY can prevent or constrain effect before commitment.

Fail-closed + evidenced

Unresolved state produces no effect, and decision/outcome can be reconstructed.

Architecture

Governed Effect Path

01

Propose

Define the exact consequence being attempted.

02

Authorize

Resolve current mandate, state, purpose, constraints and evidence.

03

Enforce

ALLOW, DENY or ESCALATE; only governed causal crossings may reach consequence.

REHT supplies fresh consequence-time authorization. RACS supplies deterministic decision semantics. Gateway/PEP enforces release. Veritas preserves evidence and receipts.
