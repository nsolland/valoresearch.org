Lower allocation did not count as savings.

The attempted compute down-allocation from 8 SMT to 6 / 4 SMT failed the required throughput/p95 boundary. EXUP rejected the cheaper configuration.

Reference allocation8 SMT
Downscale attempts6 / 4 SMT
DecisionFAILNo savings accepted

Why this matters

EXUP is not a cost-cutting rule that always selects fewer resources. A candidate only counts if it preserves the locked acceptance contract. When throughput/p95 fails, the economic improvement is inadmissible.
