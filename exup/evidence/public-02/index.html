What the evidence establishes

Open-toolchain compile/resource estimate for the internal mutable arena, with accepted full INT8 accuracy measured through host execution of the compiler-produced quantized graph. The candidate was accepted; the baseline did not meet the specified accuracy gate. The case ID in the original receipt is EXUP-HW-OPEN-01-S384.

Claim boundary

This is not a measured reduction in total device RAM, energy, latency on silicon or the cost of a commercial hardware tier. Physical silicon has not yet been validated for this case.
