18.9% less host-time for the same accepted workload.

This track translates the published median throughput in EXUP-PUBLIC-01 into capacity economics. It is a deterministic derivation, not a new benchmark.

Baseline35.10 hper 1,000,000 images
Accepted candidate28.48 hper 1,000,000 images
Capacity saving6.62 h18.9% less host-time

Derivation

PUBLIC-01 reports median throughput of 7.913766 images/s for the baseline and 9.752081 images/s for the accepted candidate. Host-time for one million images is volume ÷ throughput ÷ 3,600. The result is 35.1006 versus 28.4839 hours, a reduction of 6.6166 hours or 18.8505%.

Claim boundary

This establishes capacity/host-time reduction on the same measured host and workload. It does not establish a particular cloud bill, electricity saving or customer monetary saving. Those require the customer's actual economic baseline.
