Authority Infrastructure

As software moves from recommending to acting, authority becomes runtime infrastructure.

From access to handlingsrett

Traditional access control answers whether an identity may enter a system or use a resource. Agentic execution introduces another question: may this actor cause this exact consequence, under the current mandate, constraints and state?

VALO calls this execution boundary consequence-time authorization. Authority is resolved fresh immediately before an externally meaningful effect, and the decision is bound to evidence of what was proposed and what was allowed, denied or escalated.

Design invariantNo direct effect path: consequential actions should pass through the governed execution boundary rather than bypassing it through an ungoverned tool path.

Infrastructure, not model behaviour

The control does not depend on a model remembering a rule or choosing to comply. The enforcement point sits outside the model and evaluates explicit authority and constraints before effect.
