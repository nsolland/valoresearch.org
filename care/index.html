CARE — AI care for dogs

It’s a full-time job.

Your dog does not stop being your responsibility when you leave the house. CARE learns your dog and helps you keep track, understand, train and care — around the clock.

One dog. One model.

More than a smart collar.

The collar is the first sensor. CARE connects what happens on the dog, in the home and across its health history into one continuously learning model.

01Collar

Tracks activity, sleep, movement, routines and available health signals. Audio and motion patterns add behavioral context.

02Home

A camera and speaker add context when you are away. CARE can see what is happening and communicate through approved cues.

03Training

AI-assisted clicker logic can connect behavior, command, response and reward while keeping automation bounded and observable.

04Environment

Food and water bowls, feeders, doors, toys and even the TV can become controlled parts of the same care loop.

Health intelligence

From tracking to context.

CARE compares the dog with itself first, then with relevant dogs of similar breed, age and life stage. The goal is not to manufacture a diagnosis. It is to surface patterns early and preserve useful evidence.

CARE does not diagnose disease. It detects trends, anomalies and context that owners and professionals can evaluate.
