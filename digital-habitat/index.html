GOI · Global Operating Infrastructure

Operate globally. Know what must happen next.

GOI — Global Operating Infrastructure — brings company formation, jurisdiction and tax assessment, required filings and operating services into one operating journey. Local specialists make decisions that require licensed or statutory authority.

The model

One company journey, with obligations attached.

The founder supplies the facts once. GOI compares available formation routes, presents provider prices and timing, records the chosen structure and tracks the duties that follow. Formation, tax advice, filings and treasury are fulfilled by appropriately qualified providers.

01Establish facts

Founder residence, ownership, management, activity and intended markets become a versioned case package.

02Assess

Compare jurisdiction eligibility, formation cost, timing and likely tax and reporting obligations; flag facts that require local professional review.

03Form

Route the authorized filing to a formation provider. Track its decision, price, documents and registration status.

04Continue

Track registrations, returns, renewals and deadlines. Connect accounting, payments and treasury where eligible.

Jurisdiction & tax

Formation does not settle tax residency.

The company’s place of incorporation, where it is managed, where founders live and where it operates can create different obligations. GOI records these facts and routes uncertain cases for jurisdiction-specific tax and legal review before presenting a route as eligible.

At formationRecord the basis

Keep the founder facts, provider quote, local review, approvals and filed documents tied to the chosen route.

After formationTrack duties

Maintain a jurisdiction-specific calendar for registrations, annual filings and tax reporting, with an accountable provider for each action.

When facts changeReassess

Changes in residence, ownership, management or activity trigger a fresh review of eligibility and obligations.

Authorized execution

Local providers carry out regulated actions.

GOI assembles the case and routes each action to a qualified provider. The provider confirms its own eligibility and authority for that jurisdiction; the decision and resulting filing are linked to the exact case version.

Treasury

Treasury follows the company’s actual needs.

Bank, EMI and supported digital-asset providers are optional operating rails, subject to eligibility. GOI does not custody customer funds and does not treat a payment method as a change to tax or reporting duties.

Continuity

No single provider should own the company’s continuity.

Jurisdiction fallback and treasury fallback sit underneath the core formation product. The aim is resilience, not regulatory bypass.

JurisdictionProvider-led coverage

Jurisdictions are added when a formation provider, local obligation mapping and a support path are verified. No jurisdiction is assumed eligible for every founder.

TreasuryProvider-neutral

No single custodian, exchange, bank, stablecoin or network is intended to become permanent infrastructure.

ControlBounded authority

Local statutory roles do not receive treasury keys, platform administration or beneficial ownership merely because they fulfil a legal role.

EvidenceFail closed

If legal eligibility, authority or required evidence is missing, the action does not proceed.
