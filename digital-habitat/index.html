The model

A digital operating habitat for a company.

Digital Habitat separates legal formation, treasury, custody and fiat access. The company can be formed in one jurisdiction, hold value through regulated third-party infrastructure, and connect a bank or EMI only when needed.

01Form

Founder data, company structure and required documents are assembled into a canonical case package.

02Verify

KYC / UBO, sanctions, PEP, completeness and legal-eligibility checks run before any filing request reaches a human.

03Approve

A regulated local TCSP reviews a bounded action and returns APPROVE, DENY or ESCALATE.

04Operate

Stablecoin treasury becomes the default money layer. Fiat rails remain available for tax, payroll, cards and local settlement.

Human where legally required

Automate everything except the statutory decision.

The TCSP is not the operating system. It is the regulated approval endpoint. Digital Habitat prepares the case; the human only reviews the bounded action the law requires.

Treasury

Stablecoin first. Fiat on demand.

Digital Habitat does not custody customer funds. Regulated providers hold and settle assets. The platform handles formation orchestration, identity, policy and provider routing.

Preferred value layerUSDC / other supported stablecoins
CustodyRegulated third-party provider
FiatBank, EMI, mobile money or local rail when required

Continuity

No single provider should own the company’s continuity.

Jurisdiction fallback and treasury fallback sit underneath the core formation product. The aim is resilience, not regulatory bypass.

JurisdictionRwanda first

Rwanda is the first reference jurisdiction. Armenia, Georgia and Mauritius are being evaluated as fallback options.

TreasuryProvider-neutral

No single custodian, exchange, bank, stablecoin or network is intended to become permanent infrastructure.

ControlBounded authority

Local statutory roles do not receive treasury keys, platform administration or beneficial ownership merely because they fulfil a legal role.

EvidenceFail closed

If legal eligibility, authority or required evidence is missing, the action does not proceed.
